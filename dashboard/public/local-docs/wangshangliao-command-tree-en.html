Activities, Cards and Navigation

Lottery limits: 1-100 winners, 1-10080 minutes, and 0-10000 for capacity/invitation threshold; winners cannot exceed a nonzero capacity. Settings freeze during an active lottery. Join in the activity group only. Drawing rechecks current membership, persists random winners before one native-@ notification, and never redraws because delivery is unknown. Proactive start/result/cancellation notices are retained; they are not twelve-second command-reply cleanup. Invitation tracking starts with a roster baseline and verifies later recruits against official business attribution, not merely the native join operator. Activity points never transfer money or pay repeatedly for rejoining. Rankings use retained, trusted text for the Beijing day, twenty members per page: at most one count per member per thirty seconds, no repeated text within five minutes, excluding recorded sanctioned or recalled messages. They are not complete platform totals. 排名 and 发言排行榜 still show today's ranking and accept a page number. Automatic speech exemptions use yesterday's Beijing-day ranking: only its top twenty with more than 100 eligible texts (at least 101) newly qualify; never backfill rank21. Refresh runs every thirty seconds with a thirty-second settling delay. Existing eligibility survives day changes; each eligible text renews seven days, and seven inactive days expire it. Retained and new members may exceed twenty; this stores activity timestamps, not seven days of chat bodies. Manual grants remain unchanged. Explicit removal blocks historical readdition until a later date qualifies anew; blacklist wins and existing mutes stay in place.

Integrated mode hides the old Groups & Runtime / Moderation tabs; per-group ad and flood settings are under Activation & Rules. Card edits and banned/cancelled-account cleanup use dashboard preview jobs, complete rosters and separate action grants, never AI execution. Currently the manual Card tasks controls are mounted only in the hidden Groups & Runtime tab, with no visible integrated-mode replacement. Group Grants still exposes suffix/automatic-card settings, not manual preview, execution or stop controls. An accepted-but-unverified or unknown card result stops further writes; reconcile receipts/current membership rather than resend. Warning delivery and original-message recall are separate audit fields. The legacy N / 3 count display is neither a removal threshold nor an automatic reset. See content rules and daily schedules.
