Commands

Send 菜单 or 帮助 to open the numbered text tree. Its eight branches cover activation/identity, groups/members, rules, member/manager actions, announcements/ schedules, lotteries, invitation queries/rewards, and read-only AI. Reply with the displayed number to advance, 0 to go back, or 取消 to exit and discard unsubmitted input. Accepted operations are not reversed by exiting.

Arguments are prompted separately. Group-management mutations require a fresh 1 confirmation; backend removal, manager and schedule confirmations are still enforced. The separate 结账 flow instead requires its two-minute six-digit confirmation below. Member prompts accept 99 for the next page. Enter 不限 for a zero lottery limit/invitation threshold; a bare 0 always means back. Navigation expires after ten idle minutes, is capped at 4096 sessions, and binds the real NIM sender, bot account, conversation and group. Authorization/config changes invalidate pending mutations. Navigation numbers never simultaneously query cashback, and exiting cannot submit an old menu action. Private 取消 also ends the AI session; /chat and /exit discard pending menu input. Unopened groups still expose only restricted 开通; menu visibility does not grant data access. Cards remain dashboard-only; finance adds the separately granted private 结账 source path. Ordinary chat and AI still cannot change the ledger, and the provider-budget gate is unchanged. Menu, back-navigation and denial replies share a three-second cooldown, with silent suppression above the limit. 取消 and /exit immediately change session state even when their replies are throttled.

Group and private chats share fixed commands; the 管理 prefix is optional. Group commands bind the current group without friendship, bot mentions or private selection. Privately select with 群列表 and 选择群 1. Directory numbers expire after ten minutes and bind bot, sender, group and conversation; never reuse them across groups or conversations. Ordinary members and managers of other groups cannot manage the current group. AI consultation and personal cash-ledger queries remain private-only. Separately opted-in group conversation may answer native mentions. Send each command separately. Activation is separate from the bot's native owner/admin role. Without a verified native role, group menus, rules, member controls and activities return the same plain-language instruction to make the bot a group administrator first. Ordinary group chatter does not generate repeated notices. Moderation, activity workers and schedules stop while configuration and data remain intact. Private menus, personal cashback and group selection remain available. Selection lists only groups activated by or explicitly delegated to the verified caller under the receiving bot account. Selecting a group without bot privileges shows the same notice and blocks operation prompts, previews and execution. Framework admin status and AI quota exemptions do not expand chat authority; execution continues to verify grants, current membership and configuration versions.
