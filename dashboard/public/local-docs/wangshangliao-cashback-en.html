Limits And Operations

Manual Classification And Three-Hour Qualification

Agent pricing refers to the invitee, never the inviter. Avatar OCR and name-digit classification are removed. New invitations initially use ordinary member rules. After reviewing private proof, a cashier may explicitly switch the entry between ordinary and agent pricing. Separate group rates remain; renaming, attribution changes and later rate changes do not reprice old entries.

New invitees enter verifying with a durable deadline three hours after the first complete-roster membership observation. A successful fresh collection must confirm membership, attribution and pricing before transitioning to pending. Leaving or rejoining voids eligibility. Short restarts preserve deadlines; observation gaps over 120 seconds restart the three-hour window with an audit rather than treating unknown offline periods as continuous membership. Conversion waits for a successful scan and is not guaranteed at the exact second. Ordinary payment enforces this gate server-side. An administrator's actual-payment exception records an external fact and does not certify qualification.

Migration enables the policy for new invitations in existing groups, preserving every existing entry's amount, state, eligibility, payment and version. Existing records are not put back into qualification. New group settings use three hours.

Price Confirmation, Payment Exceptions And Existing Debt

POST /api/price-preview computes original/target cents, entry and price-policy versions, the group rule version and a digest. Repricing through /api/payments requires the complete price_confirmation; the transaction recomputes and compares it, and idempotency includes the confirmed fields. A changed rate or record requires a new preview. Old clients missing the confirmation are rejected and must refresh rather than automatically retry.

Only financial administrators may record exceptional payments. A reason and actual_payment_confirmed=true checkbox are mandatory. This may record money already paid before qualification, speech or departure checks passed; it cannot bypass recipient identity, confirmed attribution, valid prices, risk or duplicate-payment checks. Original qualification evidence and reasons are retained, and the page/audit distinguish normal registration from exceptions. Withdrawing a registration neither reverses external money nor restores void rewards; current qualification rules apply.

New-invitation collection baselines are separate from existing-debt evidence cycles. Disable/re-enable must not strand legitimate old debt. Compatible migrations add only reliably provable metadata, not guessed attribution or rewritten financial fields. The first upgrade resets source cursor/scan metadata once to recollect complete membership events, without changing old financial fields. Newer reliable departures/natural joins supersede old invitations. An individual missing-inviter source is retained and audited without poisoning valid rows. Historical concerns produce read-only review candidates, not automatic repairs, freezes or clawbacks. Contradictory evidence rejects new payments without rewriting the historical entry.
