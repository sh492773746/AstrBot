AI, Knowledge and Quota Audit: October 20

Routes and Profiles

  • Private /chat starts read-only consultation; /exit, cancellation or fifteen idle minutes end it. Account, authenticated sender and consultation role are scoped. Administrators have no AI action tools either. A new session is intended to exclude previous context, but the rejected-first-question gap below prevents an unconditional guarantee.
  • Native @, autonomous replies and idle topics all resolve profiles.resolve("group", account, group), never private consultation. They share the group persona, not members' mention histories. Master, mentions, autonomous and idle switches are separate; mentions is independent of autonomous. Saving a persona or enabling knowledge references does not enable AI. Factory master/autonomous/idle switches are off; mentions are on behind the master switch. Default hours are 09:00-23:00 Beijing; equal start/end hours mean all day.
  • Models and personas are independent selections. Total control owns separate consultation and group defaults; account/group overrides apply only to the group profile. Referenced AstrBot configurations supply allowlisted model, persona, sampling, output and history options, not tools, skills, arbitrary request bodies or external agent runners. Persona example exchanges are style samples, not actual experiences or user history.
  • The supplier gate currently stores one confirmed provider and model/address/credential binding, not universal approval for every model. Distinct personas may share it; selecting distinct model bindings does not make both routes callable. Changed models, addresses or credentials require matching gate and pricing verification.
  • Autonomous input keeps at most twelve messages for 120 seconds, coalesced over two seconds. Commands, consumed violations, unverified identities and recognized financial/private text are excluded. Lexical filters and redaction are limited safeguards, not semantic privacy proof. Private and group calls share two service-level slots with no model wait queue; each payer pool permits only one in-flight reservation.
  • Autonomous calls request response_format={"type":"json_object"} and validate silence or an exact candidate number, quote boolean and text. Only complete json or unlabelled fences are tolerated; arbitrary prose is not searched for a JSON fragment. Native delivery rechecks the selected source, identity and authority. Outcome logs contain a request digest, mode, state and reason, not message bodies. completed can mean model_silent, not a delivered group message.
  • All group output is plain text without 【群聊AI】 or 【客服咨询】 prefixes. Private read-only AI is unchanged; group personas, member histories and legacy group-consultation content never become private context.

Plans and Cost Safeguards

  • Code tiers are trial 20, Plus 2000 and Pro 5000 calls/day; displayed prices are Plus 20 USDT/30 days and Pro 50 USDT/30 days, not automated collection or upgrades. Total control previews and confirms actual CNY income; paid-cycle model-cost budgets are 70% of that receipt. Personal trial cost is capped at CNY 3/calendar month. Daily counts are ceilings, not a promise that cost budgets permit every listed call.
  • Verified identity links private use and all self-consented groups to one pool. Controller nomination is not payer consent; the payer must remain a current group manager in the same membership generation. An additional group cap is a stricter limit; zero means no additional cap, not unlimited free calls. Exemption bypasses personal/site daily counts only, not monetary, group or supplier safeguards.
  • Site trial spend is separate from paid-cycle spend; an exhausted free pool alone does not block paid plans. Factory trial-site allowance is CNY 20/month, while the site safety base starts at zero and blocks all calls until configured. Both were CNY 20 in the October 20 snapshot. The safety ceiling adds allocated budgets for paid periods overlapping that calendar month, including ended/upgraded cycles and unresolved holds; it is not one fixed CNY 20 free pool for every caller.
  • Counts/dates use Beijing time; paid cycles last thirty days. Renewal, expiry and upgrade do not erase same-day usage. Integer micro-CNY reservations cover the attested full input ceiling and bounded output, then settle validated usage. Expired prices, changed bindings, missing cost bounds or anomalous usage prevent further admission. Invalid usage retains a hold and blocks that pricing binding. This depends on the operator's truthful billable bounds, not an independent verification of supplier invoices.
  • Only provably unsent calls release reservations. HTTP errors, timeouts or unknown results do not imply free usage. Silence, invalid autonomous JSON and filtered output can still incur model cost. Unknown group calls/deliveries block automatic replay until reviewed and explicitly resumed. Quota notices go privately to the payer, not the group.

Findings and Verification Scope

The original audit findings remain below. Item 3 notes the later mention-route change; documentation updates are not implementation regressions or production acceptance:

  1. P2: A rejected first question can restore previous private context. private_interaction.py:148 clears fresh before model admission. Rejection at open_service.py:3108 or another pre-creation gate leaves the previous native conversation selected. The next question loads it with fresh=False, contradicting the fresh-consultation notice. Extracted production methods with in-memory session/conversation fakes reproduced this. Existing first-use and native-fresh tests cover normal paths separately, not the combined new /chat -> rejected first question -> successful next question.
  2. P2: Native history can lose an edit between validation and update. The check and update at native_conversations.py:148 are not compare-and-swap: an edit committed after validation but before the update can be overwritten by the old snapshot. An in-memory fake reproduced this with an independent write and asynchronous barriers, and verified that an edit completed before validation is preserved. Existing late-history tests change the record before save, not inside this interleaving. Separately, both AI routes check native selection/history before HTTP, not again before delivery. Existing documentation/tests promise pre-write checking, not cancellation on dashboard deletion, switching or editing; no explicit intentional policy was found either. Late delivery alone is therefore a contract question, not a separately confirmed P2 defect or evidence of an authorization bypass. It is distinct from the reproduced lost update.
  3. P2, pre-change finding: Group history options had inconsistent semantics. The original AST audit of group_ai.py:570, native load and payload construction found candidate truncation without previous-turn group input. Native @ now uses the separate key and bounded configured history above, with autonomous buffers unchanged. The old reproduction is not evidence against the new mention payload. Implementation regressions must still confirm zero/one/multiple turns, isolation across members/groups/accounts/private chats and no legacy-history reuse. Configuration success or dashboard visibility is not multi-turn acceptance.
  4. Unmeasured performance risk: major knowledge writes, reservations and settlement use workers, but admission/delivery guards still call synchronous profile, knowledge-version, payer and SQLite reads (group_ai.py:655, open_service.py:3213). Not all AI database IO is asynchronous. Slow-disk/lock-wait delay was not measured here.

Initial Changes

  • Deduplicate by account, group and message before the registered plugin observer enriches a trusted envelope. Commit its content, sender lane and initial routing state together before acknowledging receipt.
  • Group messages are ordered per real sender; private sessions retain their original ordering. Twelve workers serve ordinary messages and four are reserved for fixed commands. Classification applies only after selecting each lane's oldest message, so commands cannot overtake their sender. Backlog remains in the inbox.
  • Review concurrency is four per robot with a default twelve-second whole-review deadline. Customer concurrency is two per robot, with a conversation lease acquired before history loading and released at final event completion. Violations cannot enter customer chat.
  • Flood thresholds are three messages in five seconds, eight in ten seconds, or five equal normalized texts in thirty seconds. Both trusted event time and receive time are checked. Events delayed over ten seconds or over two seconds in the future are not live flood evidence.
  • Flood sanctions attempt the mute first, then recall every authenticated burst message, bypassing the ordinary three-recall grace period. A continuing burst gets one mute; later spam messages are still recalled. Durable batch and individual receipts prevent blind retries of unknown outcomes. Unrelated members and historical messages are excluded. Plugin keyword_defaults.json seeds missing fields in new policies, preserving custom lists, legacy keywords and explicitly empty lists.
  • The former "Removal keywords" control is now "High-risk keywords (no auto-kick)", with a matching audit category label. Persisted kick_keywords, historical evidence and the automatic-removal prohibition remain unchanged.
  • Windows retain at most 4096 members and 256 digests per member. Plugin reload restores recent durable live evidence from the last thirty seconds without resetting sanction history.
  • Ordinary reviews may reuse a complete directory for fifteen seconds. Action execution still forces verification. Concurrent refreshes coalesce; failure invalidates usable cache without inventing a complete roster or deleting known identity mappings.
  • Semantic/content review IO runs on database workers, initializing each repository schema once. Other synchronous repositories, including progressive execution and activities, still require follow-up migration.
  • Card jobs are indexed by account, group and state. Empty automatic scans do not create durable jobs; uncertain outcomes still block duplicate mutations.
