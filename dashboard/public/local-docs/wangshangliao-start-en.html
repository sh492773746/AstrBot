Production Account And Group Selection

Group selectors include only accounts currently loaded by the service. Historical groups of stopped test accounts remain stored but are not production choices. Other bot selectors also omit explicitly disabled instances. If an account disappears on refresh, clear the selection, previews and member data rather than switching silently to another account's similarly named group. This deployment uses Xiaoman, 43506527; filtering never deletes accounts, settings or audits.

Selecting a group does not activate moderation. A pending group needs verified native bot authority and an eligible native manager, followed by an Activation preview and confirmation before its Rules can operate. Failed or oversized rosters cannot be replaced with partial evidence. The member-directory route has its own bounded 3MiB response allowance; authentication, decompression, identity and complete-pagination validation remain mandatory. Ordinary routes retain 1MiB.

For a temporary management-interface 502, wait and refresh its read-only status. After a save, blacklist command or mute, verify the outcome before submitting again: a gateway error does not prove that the original operation was never submitted. These operations are not automatically retried. Distinguish rejected platform-directory responses from proxy connection failures during restarts; an online bot alone does not establish that the management page works.

Group Join Welcomes

New source entrance: Wangshangliao Moderation → Groups → Rules → Select Group → 入群欢迎. Off by default. Enter up to300 characters, enable, then Preview Changes and Confirm Save. Native mentions are prepended automatically; do not type a nickname or UID as identity. This independent setting does not reschedule advertisements or change lists, sanctions or finance.

Only fresh native admissions corroborated with the latest membership event and complete roster qualify. Applications, user text, old notifications and duplicate joins do not. A two-second window coalesces simultaneous admissions into one message, including three or more arrivals (one or two arrivals in that window are also combined). At most20 members are mentioned, subject to the native message-size limit; larger batches include a total rather than being split into a burst. Bots and blacklisted members are excluded.

Accepted welcomes get a durable20-second self-recall timer, not per-second edits or messages. After a second welcome is accepted, the previous welcome becomes due for recall. A failed or uncertain second send never prematurely supersedes the previous message. Only this bot's welcome receipts qualify; ordinary member messages and ads are untouched. Unknown sends/recalls are not automatically retried. Disconnections and queue timing mean the phone is not guaranteed to remove the message at precisely the twentieth second. Pause, disabling or authority changes fence new submissions; old admissions are not replayed.
