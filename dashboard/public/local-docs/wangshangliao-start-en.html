In-Group Blacklist Release

An administrator mentions one member using the real client selector, or replies to their verifiable same-group message, and sends 解除黑名单 (alias 移除黑名单). The bot removes the entry and attempts to unmute, reporting both results. Private-chat and Dashboard removal only remove the entry; independent unmuting does not remove it. Do not retry an unknown outcome. Older dated removal-only notes do not describe this current in-group command.

Daily Speech Activity (October 26, 2026)

Open Wangshangliao Management → Groups → Speech Activity, select the bot and group, then choose today or yesterday. Beijing midnight starts a new daily count; only the previous and current dates are retained, not a long-term activity history. Group totals include active members, message types, hourly distribution and top 30. Search a member name or UID and select the matching member for separate daily statistics. Recalled/violating messages, bots and commands do not count; the newest 30 seconds await verification. Automatic whitelist eligibility is unchanged. The copy icon beside the ranking copies the selected date's top 30 with group name/ID, date, rank, member name/UID and message count. Empty or incomplete rankings cannot be copied. A browser denial displays an error, never a false success.

Bots with a configured Focused Speech Report also show exact-UID member totals by configured group, per-member combined totals and the combined team total. The Today/Yesterday selection applies to this report; its copy icon includes the complete group breakdown. Only effective text counts as chat sentences: images, videos, forwards, recalled/violating messages, bots and commands are excluded. The newest 30 seconds await verification, and only today/yesterday are retained. Incomplete reports cannot be copied. A disabled configured group blocks the whole report rather than silently shrinking its scope. Reports do not post into groups or change whitelist/moderation settings.

2. Activate A Group

  1. A current native group owner or administrator invites the bot.
  2. That verified inviter sends 开通 in the group; friendship is unnecessary.
  3. After activation, make the bot a native group administrator.
  4. Send 菜单 or 规则 to check state. Advertising and flood rules default on, but execution still requires native privileges and separate action grants.

Ordinary-member invitations trigger controlled departure. Never substitute the owner, approver or notification sender for the real inviter. Unknown sources remain unopened and require operator investigation. Dashboard claim codes are single-use, digest-only and valid for ten minutes; they do not bypass invitation or inviter-role verification. Reinvitation cannot steal existing ownership.

Without native bot privileges, group features instruct the user to make the bot a group administrator first. Repeating commands does not grant that role.

Manual/current automatic whitelist entries, native owners/administrators and managed bots are exempt. Ordinary images, stickers, merged forwards and videos in groups without this switch are not recalled or muted solely for their media type. Supported authenticated media messages count toward flood thresholds; media is not downloaded or interpreted, and text, voice and file names are not guessed to be images or videos. Administrators and protected bots remain exempt; messages predating rule activation are not penalized.

Flood rules detect five texts/supported media messages from one member in three seconds, fifteen in ten seconds, or five repeated texts in thirty seconds. After warning grace, mute first, then recall the matching burst without waiting for AI. Accepted does not mean confirmed successful; uncertain operations must be reconciled, not blindly repeated.

Before three accepted advertising recalls, a failed or unknown recall earns no warning and causes no mute. After that grace, recalls and mutes are independent and authorization is checked again. An unknown mute freezes later automatic mutes, but does not itself disable separately authorized recalls. Manual recalls and mutes never increment automatic penalty counts.

Split-contact rules exclude already recalled evidence and evidence with an accepted, verified, unknown or sending recall receipt. This applies to solicitation text, broadcast cues, earlier numbers and the current number. Native notices must match this account, group, message, original client and native sender; a rejected receipt is not proof of recall. These evidence checks do not add abuse/image recognition, longer mutes, or automatic removal.

Card batches and banned/cancelled-member cleanup are dashboard-only. They are not friend deletion. Authorization, membership, state and revisions are rechecked immediately before submission.
