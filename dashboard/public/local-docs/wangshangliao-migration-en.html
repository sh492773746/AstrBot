Storage and lifecycle

The account-bound policy store is data/plugin_data/wangshangliao_moderation/settings.sqlite3. It stores base-policy revisions, effective times and change history. Public tenants, grants, group AI and quotas live in wangshangliao_open/open-service.sqlite3 and overlay that snapshot at runtime. Old instance policies must not overwrite tenant rules. Web controls and authorized fixed commands use their respective versioned writers. Public conversational AI has no configuration or management write tools; the old administrator-AI writer description is historical. Activity, cash and execution ledgers stay at their original paths. Missing migrated policies fail closed, never import empty defaults. Legacy requests cannot combine transport changes and business edits.

Disabling the plugin revokes the runtime generation, test windows and queued replies, but does not log out the account. Delayed events cannot send or use management tools. Cold starts quarantine old pending chat commands, preserving invitation notices separately. Read-only restoration of observation groups cannot activate business execution.

Only currently authorized fixed private management commands can preview and confirm changes. AI explains commands but cannot execute them. Private consultation requires /chat. Group mentions, including administrator mentions, require per-group opt-in and, like autonomous replies and idle topics, resolve profiles.resolve("group", account, group), never private consultation. They share the group persona, with account/group overrides, but verified members' mention histories remain independent and bounded by the configured turns. Other members, groups, accounts, private chats and old group-consultation history are never mixed, migrated or reused. Autonomous candidate buffers and the original /group origin remain unchanged. All group output is plain text without 【群聊AI】 or 【客服咨询】 prefixes; private read-only AI is unchanged. mentions remains independent of autonomous. Ordinary chat reaches bounded, filtered model context only when autonomous conversation is separately enabled; idle speech is another switch. Budget, payer confirmation, current permissions, quotas and hours apply. Existing AstrBot identity, profile, persona and knowledge-base systems remain authoritative.

Pausing, disconnecting or failing to read a directory does not prove departure. Verified departure and a durable generation drive exact-group working-data cleanup, retaining finance and uncertain-action evidence. Cleanup commits per database, not as a cross-database atomic transaction.

Friend reads have a minimum three-second interval and approvals a minimum thirty-second interval, alongside scan periods and durable read backoff. Backup and rollback

Run in a controlled maintenance window with business writes stopped:

PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python scripts/wsl_policy_migrate.py backup \
  --data /opt/astrbot-prod/data --output /secure-backups/wsl-before-migration
PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python scripts/wsl_policy_migrate.py export \
  --data /opt/astrbot-prod/data --output /secure-backups/wsl-latest-config.json

Both destinations must be new. Backups include SQLite integrity checks, SHA-256 manifests, core configuration and sealed credentials. Keep these private. Individual online SQLite backups are not a cross-database atomic snapshot; stop writes for cutover consistency. Coordinate both shared AstrBot and the independent cashier. Verify that the manifest includes native storage and centralized wangshangliao_moderation, wangshangliao_open and wangshangliao_cashback data, account-directory bindings, friend approvals, onboarding claims, model settings and Access side files. Business directories are account-bound; sealed sessions remain instance-bound and may be elsewhere. The older wangshangliao_maintenance.py backup omits centralized business stores and is not a complete integrated backup. Back up global personas, knowledge bases and AstrBot conversations separately. Rollback export uses the latest account-matched policies, never restores financial ledgers and never overwrites live files. Do not replace post-migration payment history with old backups. Export covers base policies, not conversion of public tenants, group AI, plans, budgets or scoped knowledge. A successful export does not prove a lossless downgrade. Do not restore the retired public socket or AI write tools during rollback.

Replacing the cashback collector

Deleting a bot does not delete the independent cashback ledger, but the ledger still belongs to the retired collector. Do not just overwrite its instance ID. Back up and stop writes before an administrator calls migration.migrate_cashback_collector; it is not exposed to chat, AI tools or cashiers. Require positive target membership and a complete roster fetched within five minutes. Stale group versions, bindings or conflicting member identities reject the entire transaction.

Existing entry IDs, inviter attribution, amounts, payment registration and historical prices remain intact. Evidence retains its original collecting account; group- and target-account-scoped collector_migrations authorize only pre-cutoff history. Subsequent transfers cannot extend earlier cutoffs. Newly baselined roster members are ineligible, with zero reward. Restarting preserves cashier-confirmed manual agent prices.

Set the shared access.json collector_id and select the group in the target bot's business policy without copying sanction permissions. A fresh real roster/source scan is required before settlement. Retain other groups' history without granting access when target membership is unconfirmed.
