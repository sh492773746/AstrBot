Friend pacing additionally retains the pre-dispatch intent and persists the completion timestamp after success, failure or cancellation. Subsequent requests wait at least three seconds and approvals thirty seconds. Long shared-HTTP-lock waits cannot consume that cooldown early, and no sleep is added inside that lock. Twelve barrier/virtual-clock regressions cover slow calls, failures, repeated cancellation and restart.

Only a backend administrator may register an external payment already made, with both explicit actual-payment confirmation and a reason. Waiting, speech and departure conditions may be waived for registration, but recipient identity, confirmed attribution, price, risk and idempotency may not. Original qualification evidence and exception reasons remain audited. Responses and paid rows identify exception registration; withdrawal does not revive invalidated reward qualification.

Browser checks found that exception labels compared two independently sampled timestamps. Classification now precisely matches structured payment evidence from the same entry's latest payment audit, without a tolerance window. Real-clock regressions cover pagination, reader reuse, withdrawal and re-registration. Cross-review also repaired accepted announcements being misclassified by later readback errors and private data leaking after revocation. The actual query freezes its original group scope through the send lock and every fragment, rather than guessing from the current selection or command at send time. This includes numbered menus, pagination and back navigation.
